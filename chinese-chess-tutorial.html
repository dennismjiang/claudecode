Quick Reference - All Pieces

General (帥/將)

Moves 1 space orthogonally, stays in palace

Advisor (仕/士)

Moves 1 space diagonally, stays in palace

Elephant (相/象)

Moves 2 spaces diagonally, cannot cross river

Horse (傌/馬)

L-shape move, can be blocked

Chariot (俥/車)

Moves any distance orthogonally

Cannon (炮/砲)

Moves orthogonally, jumps to capture

Soldier (兵/卒)

Forward only, sideways after crossing river
